> Read SPEC.md. Section 12 is the build order, Step 0 through Step 12 plus 4A, 4B, 4C

for the TypeScript engine. They run. Check the TypeScript against them rather than
against your memory of what the rules say.

## The one thing most likely to go wrong

Regenerating the rule packs. They took real work, they encode findings from the source
documents that are easy to get backwards, and running them found 19 defects that were
invisible on the page. If Claude Code offers to rewrite them, say no and point it at
`docs/cisa-2026-elements.md`.
